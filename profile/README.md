## MIMO Technology

We build **[Zenit](https://zenit.aero)**, a web app for practising cockpit procedures before you fly.

Pilots rehearse checklists in an interactive 360° photo of the cockpit they actually fly. They tap the real switches in the right order, get instant feedback, and drill normal and emergency procedures at home, so the flows are automatic before the next flight.

- **Student pilots** come to each lesson already knowing the cockpit.
- **Private and club pilots** stay current between flights and across aircraft types.
- **Flight schools** build cockpits and checklists for their own fleet.

**Stack:** TypeScript · Next.js · NestJS · Prisma · PostgreSQL · Google Cloud
